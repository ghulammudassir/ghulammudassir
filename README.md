## 🚀 About Me

I'm a **Assistant Professor, Artificial Intelligence Researcher, and Data Scientist** with extensive international experience in higher education, academic research, and interdisciplinary collaboration.

Currently, I serve as a **Lecturer in Computing at the University of Buckingham, UK**, and as a collaborative lecturer at **Guangzhou Panyu Polytechnic, China**.

I earned my **PhD in Applied Data Sciences from the University of L'Aquila, Italy**, where my doctoral research focused on Deep Reinforcement Learning for post-disaster reconstruction planning.

My research explores the development of intelligent, explainable, trustworthy, and efficient AI systems, with applications in healthcare, disaster management, software engineering, and large language models.

I am passionate about international research collaboration, academic mentoring, and translating advanced AI research into practical solutions.
